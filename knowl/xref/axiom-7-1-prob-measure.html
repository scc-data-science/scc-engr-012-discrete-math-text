Axiom 7.1.2 Probability Axioms.

Let \(\mathbb{P}\) be a probability measure on a sample space \(\Omega\text{.}\)
  1. (Nonnegativity) \(\mathbb{P}(A) \geq 0\) for every event \(A\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. (Additivity) If \(A\) and \(B\) are disjoint events (that is, \(A \cap B = \varnothing\)), then the probability of their union satisfies
    \begin{equation*} \mathbb{P}(A \cup B) = \mathbb{P}(A) + \mathbb{P}(B). \end{equation*}
    More generally, if \(A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n\) is a sequence of disjoint events, then:
    \begin{equation*} \mathbb{P} \left( \bigcup_{k=1}^n A_k \right) = \sum_{k=1}^n \mathbb{P}(A_k). \end{equation*}
    Note that \(n\) need not be finite.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. (Normalization) The probability of the entire sample space is 1; that is, \(\mathbb{P}(\Omega) = 1\text{.}\)
