Theorem 7.4.5.

We say that events \(A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n\) are independent if
\begin{equation*} \mathbb{P} \left( \bigcap_{k \in S} A_k \right) = \prod_{k \in S} \mathbb{P}(A_k), \end{equation*}
where \(S\) represents all subsets of \(\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}\) with at least two elements.
