Example 5.5.2 Division by \(2\) modulo \(5\).

We know that \(\mathrm{gcd}(2, 5) = 1\text{,}\) so \(2^{-1}\) exists modulo \(5\text{.}\) What is it?
🔗
Solution.
One way to do this is to guess and check.
🔗
\begin{align*} 2 (1) \amp\equiv 2, \\ 2 (2) \amp\equiv 4, \\ 2 (3) \amp\equiv 1. \end{align*}
🔗
Hence, \(2^{-1} = 3\) (mod \(5\)).
