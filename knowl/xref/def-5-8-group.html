Definition 5.8.6.

A group is a nonempty set \(G\) equipped with a binary operation \(\times\text{,}\) that maps pairs of elements of \(G\) to an element of \(G\text{,}\) that satisfies:
🔗
  1. Closure: For every \(g_1, g_2 \in G\text{,}\) \(g_1 \cdot g_2 \in G\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Associativity: For every \(g_1, g_2, g_3 \in G\text{,}\) \((g_1 \cdot g_2) \cdot g_3 = g_1 \cdot (g_2 \cdot g_3)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Identity: There exists an element \(e \in G\) such that \(e \cdot g = g \cdot e = g\) for any \(g \in G\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Inverse: For every \(g \in G\text{,}\) there exists \(g' \in G\) such that \(g \cdot g' = g' \cdot g = e\text{,}\) the identity element in \(G\text{.}\)
