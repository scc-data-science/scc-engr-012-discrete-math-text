Theorem 6.4.1.

Let \(x, y\) be real numbers and \(n\) be a positive integer. Then,
\begin{equation*} (x + y)^n = \sum_{k = 0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} x^{k} y^{n - k}. \end{equation*}
where
\begin{equation*} \binom{n}{k} = \frac{n!}{k! \cdot (n - k)!} \end{equation*}
is the binomial coefficient.
