Theorem 6.1.2 The Addition Rule.

Suppose there is a sequence of \(r\) events, \(E_1, E_2, \ldots, E_r\) such that:
  1. There are \(n_i\) outcomes associated with event \(E_i\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. No two events can occur simultaneously.
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Then, there are \(n_1 + n_2 + \cdots + n_r\) ways in which one of the \(r\) events in the sequence can occur.
