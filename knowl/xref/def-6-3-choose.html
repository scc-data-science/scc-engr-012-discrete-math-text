Definition 6.3.2.

Let \(n\) and \(r\) be nonnegative integers with \(r \leq n\text{.}\) The binomial coefficient \(\binom{n}{k}\) is the number of ways to choose \(k\) unordered items from a set of \(n\) distinct total items:
\begin{equation*} \binom{n}{k} = C(n, r) = \frac{n!}{r! \cdot (n - r)!}. \end{equation*}
We read \(\binom{n}{k}\) as “\(n\) choose \(k\text{.}\)”
