Theorem 5.7.3 Sunzi’s Theorem.

Let \(m_1, m_2, \ldots, m_k \geq 2\) be pairwise coprime. Suppose \(a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_k \in \mathbb{Z}\text{.}\) Then:
🔗
\begin{align*} x \equiv \amp \; a_1 \pmod{m_1}\\ x \equiv \amp \; a_2 \pmod{m_2}\\ \vdots \amp \; \\ x \equiv \amp \; a_k \pmod{m_k} \end{align*}
🔗
  1. Has a solution.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. If \(x\) and \(y\) are both solutions, then \(x \equiv y \pmod{\prod_{j=1}^k m_j}\text{.}\)
