Definition 7.8.1.

Let \(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n\) be the support of a random variable \(X\text{.}\) Then, the variance of \(X\text{,}\) denoted by \(\mathrm{Var}[X]\text{,}\) is defined by:
\begin{equation*} \mathrm{Var}[X] = \mathbb{E} \left[(X - \mathbb{E}[X])^2 \right] = \sum_{k=1}^n (x_k - \mathbb{E}[X])^2 \cdot \mathbb{P}(X = x_k). \end{equation*}
The standard deviation of \(X\text{,}\) denoted by \(\mathrm{sd}[X]\text{,}\) is \(\mathrm{sd}[X] = \sqrt{\mathrm{Var}[X]}\text{.}\)
