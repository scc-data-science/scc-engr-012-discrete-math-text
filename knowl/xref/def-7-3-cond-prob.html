Definition 7.3.1 Conditional Probability.

Let \(A\) and \(B\) be events in a sample space \(\Omega\) such that \(A \subseteq B\text{.}\) Then, the conditional probability of \(A\) given \(B\) is:
\begin{equation*} \mathbb{P}(A \mid B) = \frac{\text{Number of elements of $A \cap B$}}{\text{Number of elements of $B$}} = \frac{\mathbb{P}(A \cap B)}{\mathbb{P}(B)}. \end{equation*}
