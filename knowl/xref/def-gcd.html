Definition 5.3.5.

Let \(a\) and \(b\) be integers. The greatest common divisor of \(a\) and \(b\) is an integer satisfying the following properties:
🔗
  1. \(d \gt 0\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(d \mid a\) and \(d \mid b\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Whenever \(e \mid a\) and \(e \mid b\text{,}\) we have \(e \mid d\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
We write \(d = \mathrm{gcd}(a, b)\text{.}\) If \(\mathrm{gcd}(a, b) = 1\text{,}\) we say that \(a\) and \(b\) are relatively prime.
