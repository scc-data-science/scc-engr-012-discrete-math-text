Definition 5.8.1.

Let \(\mathbb{Z} / m \mathbb{Z} = \{0, 1, \ldots, m - 1\}\) be the set of integers modulo \(m\text{,}\) equipped with the following definitions of addition (\(+\)) and multiplication (\(\cdot\)):
🔗
\begin{gather*} a + b := a + b \pmod m\\ a \cdot b := a \cdot b \pmod m \end{gather*}
🔗
for any \(a, b \in \mathbb{Z} / m \mathbb{Z}\text{.}\) We read \(\mathbb{Z} / m \mathbb{Z}\) as “Z mod \(m\) Z” or say "the integers modulo \(m\text{.}\)"
🔗
It is common to abbreviate and write \(\mathbb{Z}_m := \mathbb{Z} / m \mathbb{Z}\text{.}\)
