Lemma 5.1.4.

For all integers \(a\) and \(b\text{,}\) if \(a\) and \(b\) are positive and \(a \mid b\text{,}\) then \(a \leq b\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.

Suppose \(a\) and \(b\) are positive integers such that \(a \mid b\text{.}\) Then, there exists an integer \(k\) such that \(b = ka\text{.}\) Moreover, \(k > 0\) (and hence \(k \geq 1\)) since \(a, b > 0\text{.}\) Therefore, \(b = ka \geq 1 \cdot a = a\text{.}\)
